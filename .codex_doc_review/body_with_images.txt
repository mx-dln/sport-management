Chapter I
Introduction
Project Context
In the current generation, technology has become very essential part of life that helps different organizations establish effective methods that could improve and elevate the way they communicate and manage information with ease. This is especially vital in sports management since there are different kinds of data, which must be organized and updated regularly. Athlete registration, records, and training, plus announcements are some of the information that are normally handled by the coach or administrator of a sports team. It is not easy to organize and update paper-based records of different types of information, hence the need to design and develop a web-based system to address this limitation.
A Web-Based Sports Management Information System with SMS Notification is proposed so that sports-related information, such as trainings, games, schedules, and important announcements can be managed digitally, which can make it easier and more efficient for coaches and administrators to update and monitor the records and needs of the athletes in their school or organization. This is because the SMS notification feature can also be used to relay important information, which can reach the intended recipients through their mobile phones.
In addition to this, the proposed system can also be utilized to improve the way the sports organization manages and monitors its records and information. It can ease the process of updating and viewing the data without the use of paper and pencil since everything is organized in a database. SMS notification can also help inform the students or athletes of a particular
sports team regarding their training schedules, meetings, updates, and other important information.
Therefore, this study will design and develop a Web-Based Sports Management Information System with SMS Notification to provide a more efficient and reliable way of managing information and interacting with each other within a sports organization.
Objectives of the Study
The primary goal of this study is to Design, Develop, and Implement a Web-based Sports Management Information System with SMS Notification that will enhance the management and organization of the information of the athletes and provide a more efficient way of communicating to them.
Specifically, this study aims to:
1. Develop a web-based Sports Management Information System for managing athlete profiles, attendance, schedules, and performance records.
2. Integrate an SMS notification feature for timely communication with athletes and sports staff.
3. Evaluate the system using selected ISO/IEC 25010:2023 software quality characteristics.
a. Functional Suitability;
b. Usability;
c. Reliability; and
d. Security;
Scope and Limitations
This study aimed to design and develop a web-based sports management information system with SMS notification for the Sports Department of Isabela State University. This proposed system would address the needs of coaches and sports administrators in terms of managing sports-related data conveniently and efficiently. It would provide an online platform that allows the authorized users to store, update, and track information pertaining to the athlete’s profile, attendance, training, sports events,and other pertinent records.
The proposed system will only handle the management of ISU Athletes and SMS notification for sports-related activities. It will not incorporate other essential functions, such as biometric and other wearable devices, online registration for external sports events,financial management, and others. The application’s success relies on cellular network providers access and the accuracy of the contact details of the users provided in the system database. Moreover, it needs internet access to update and access the database.

Technical Background
The Sports Office in Isabela State University (ISU) deals with various information about the athletes such as profile, training, medical records, participation lists, attendance, and performances. The Sports Office has been manually handling this kind of information which sometimes requires a long time in processing and updating data records. This is why a database is necessary to make the process of the Sports Office more efficient and convenient in terms of managing and retrieving information.
The proposed Web-Based Sports Management Information System with SMS Notification will provide a web application as a platform to store and manage sports records. The Sports Office will be assisted by the system in terms of facilitating and organizing the information of the athletes with the help of a database where the data can be easily accessed, updated, and viewed.
The system will also include an SMS notification system that will aid the Sports Office in informing the athletes about sports-related concerns such as schedule, competitions, announcements, and other concerns. This will assist in reducing the time the Sports Office spends in communicating with the athletes.

Software Development Methodology
[IMAGE 9: media/image9.jpg]
Figure 9. Spiral Model of Web-Based Sports Management Information System with SMS Notification
The figure below represents a process of developing a web-based solution for athlete and event tracking with SMS communication. It can be seen that the system development is an iterative process involving development, testing, and improving the product until the end-users are satisfied.
Phase 1: Planning
In this stage, the team establishes the objectives of the system under consideration. For instance, the main purpose of this project is to develop a web-based system that monitors and tracks athletes and events while notifying the users via SMS. In addition, the team determines the stakeholders, which include athletes, coaches, administrators, and parents.
Phase 2: Risk Analysis
In this phase, the team conducts a risk analysis to examine the possible problems that may arise during the project implementation. For instance, the project may encounter such issues as data confidentiality, API and SMS challenges, and end-users’ adoption. Ideally, the team comes up with risk responses and long-term solutions to these problems. For instance, the team can address data confidentiality by considering data hosting services that are reliable and protected from external attacks.
Phase 3: Engineering / Development
At this stage, the team develops the system in accordance with the objectives set in Phase 1. For instance, the system will be designed to host web applications, manage and monitor athletes’ performance, and have a dashboard module that depicts the progress of each athlete. In addition, the system will offer features such as user accounts, SMS alerts, event management tools, and reports.
Phase 4: Testing and Evaluation
At this juncture, the team performs a test to establish the validity and reliability of the new system in terms of performance, security, and usability. For instance, the systems can be subjected to an end-to-end data encryption test to determine its resistance to attacks and SMS deliverability. The stakeholders such as coaches, administrators, and athletes participate in the testing process and give their recommendations.
Phase 5: Iteration / Improvement
The feedback gained from the testing phase guides the improvement of the system under development so that it meets the initial objectives. For instance, the feedback may reveal flaws that need to be addressed or new features to add, for instance, graphs and updated SMS reports. Then, the project team goes back to the planning phase, and the project cycle repeats itself until a quality product is achieved.

Observation
During the observation, the researchers discovered that record management, schedules, and announcements are being done manually, which creates difficulties in managing and coordinating the activities of the participants. Information dissemination on issues related to training, events, and sports activities is hindered due to the lack of effective information communication mechanisms, such as SMS notification. The researchers also discovered that the coaches and administrators have faced many challenges with record management and monitoring of sports activities. As a result, the researchers recommended the development of a web-based system with SMS notification to facilitate faster information dissemination, improved record management, and enhanced coordination of the activities.
Interviews
The researchers interviewed selected coaches, athletes, and sports coordinators to understand the current situation and processes regarding the management of sports activities and records. In the course of conversation, the respondents explained that mostly everything is currently done manually, resulting in problems with up-to-date information and relevant announcements getting lost in the process. They also expressed the need for a faster and more systematic means of communication, especially in terms of sharing relevant news, information, and updates concerning the team’s training schedules and events. After compiling the information gathered from the respondents, the researchers concluded that a Web-based sports management information system with SMS notification would be of great help in terms of managing and disseminating sports-related announcements and records.
Questionnaire
The researchers adopted a questionnaire to gather data and receive feedback from athletes, coaches, and administrators concerning the issue of sports functions and communication. The questionnaire was designed to identify the primary concerns regarding the record, schedules, announcements, and updates of the sports events. The participants responded to the questionnaire by sharing their experiences and challenges in record management, communication breakdown, and the possible solution to these problems. The researchers received valuable information that led them to develop the Web-Based Sports Management Information System with SMS Notification to address the issue and handle all aspects of recording and communication efficiently.

Different Software Testing
Testing
This testing phase will ensure that the system is fit to serve its intended purpose. In this way, it can guarantee that handling sports information and dissemination will be faster, more efficient, and organized for all stakeholders.
Unit Testing
Every aspect of the system will be individually verified including the login function, athlete data, scheduling, and SMS notifications, which ensures that each system operates effectively on an individual basis.
Compatibility Testing
In this phase, the system will undergo a test on multiple devices including desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and mobile phones. It will also be tested on various web browsers like Chrome, Firefox, and Edge to ensure that it displays and operates correctly on different platforms.
Performance Testing
The purpose of the test is to examine the system's stability and speed when a large number of users are accessing it at the same time, and also to assess how quickly and efficiently the system sends out SMS messages.
User Acceptance Testing
The system will likewise be assessed by real users such as coaches and administrators. Feedback from them is important in order to find out whether the system is useful, easy to understand, and meets their daily needs in the area of sports management.
Security Testing
Finally, the system will be checked to ensure that all the data is safe and secure. Access to important information such as athlete records will be restricted to authorized users only.

System Architecture
[IMAGE 10: media/image10.jpeg]
Figure 9. System Architecture of Isabela State University – Cauayan Sports Borrowing System with SMS Notification
It is here that all information is stored, including the athletes’ names, their stats, and the game schedules. So, when a coach decides to make a change, the brain executes it right away. These are the authorities. By means of their laptops, they “speak” to the brain in order to update the records or let it know that a particular practice was moved to a different field. Once the latter learns about the changes, it sends a digital “shout” via the router. The SMS Gateway is like a translator that converts the brain’s output into text messages so that they can be delivered via air. This is the ultimate goal. In this way, even if an athlete is off the grid or doesn’t have access to data or Wi-Fi, he/she will still be notified via SMS. It’s guaranteed that no one will miss a thing, and no one will show up at an abandoned gym.
